My Story

Sixteen years of holding space for new beginnings.

The Beginning

Finding my calling

Some paths choose us rather than the other way around. At nineteen, I discovered what a doula was, and something awakened in me — a recognition so profound it felt like remembering rather than learning.

Now, after sixteen years supporting families through life's most transformative transition, I understand that this path was always waiting for me.

Lineage

Rooted in ancestry, shaped by presence.

My work is shaped by my Sinixt, Skoyelpi, and Interior Salish lineage — an inheritance of slow attention, deep listening, and the understanding that postpartum is not a moment but a season worthy of sanctuary.

I bring this lineage into the homes I serve through every gesture: how I move in your space, how I hold your baby, how I listen to what you are not yet able to say. The fourth trimester is sacred ground. I treat it that way.
