My Approach

Slow, sovereign, attuned.

Care that meets you where you are — without rush, without judgment.

The fourth trimester is a season, not a moment.

My approach is built on a quiet belief: that the postpartum chapter deserves as much attention and reverence as birth itself. The way we tend to the beginning shapes the years that follow.

I weave evidence-based newborn care with ancestral wisdom, body-led attunement, and a deep respect for your family's own knowing. You are the expert on your child. I am here to hold the room steady while you remember that.

Core Beliefs

What guides me into every home I enter.

The postpartum period deserves as much attention and care as birth itself.
