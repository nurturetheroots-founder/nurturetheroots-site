Services

Care, shaped to you.

Postpartum support designed around your family's rhythm — not the other way around.

In-Home Postpartum Support

Those early weeks can feel both magical and overwhelming. I create a nurturing sanctuary inside your home — hands-on recovery support, emotional companionship, light meal care, and practical help that keeps your household running while you rest.

Overnight Care

Restful nights for growing families. I tend to your baby through the night with feeding support, gentle soothing, and diaper changes — all handled with quiet, loving attention while you sleep.

Newborn Care & Soothing

Drawing on specialized training in newborn development, I help you understand your baby's cues and create the early rhythms of feeding, sleep, and connection.

Lactation & Feeding Support

Patient, evidence-based guidance through breastfeeding, bottle-feeding, pumping, or any combination. We move at your pace, and we honor whatever path is right for your family.

Tailored Consultations

Focused, hour-long sessions for the specific questions or decisions you are sitting with right now — sleep, feeding, returning to work, sibling transitions, or anything else that's pressing.

Family Bonding Support

Gentle guidance for nurturing connection across every member of your family — partners, older children, chosen family — with sensitivity to every kind of family structure.
