My Story

My Journey to
Nurture The Roots

Some paths choose us rather than the other way around. At just 19 years old, I discovered what a doula was — and something awakened within me. A recognition so profound it felt like remembering rather than learning.

This wasn't a career I found through careful planning. It was a calling that resonated in the deepest parts of who I am. Now, after 15+ years supporting families through life's most transformative transitions, I know this journey was always waiting for me.
