Frequently Asked

Gentle answers to common questions.

If something is missing, please reach out — I'm happy to talk.

What is a postpartum doula?

A postpartum doula is a trained professional who supports families during the early weeks and months after birth — through hands-on newborn care, lactation support, emotional companionship, household ease, and steady presence. My work is non-medical, deeply relational, and centered on what your family actually needs.

What areas of San Francisco do you serve?

I serve families across San Francisco and select parts of the surrounding Bay Area. If you are unsure whether you are in my service area, please reach out and ask.

What does an overnight shift look like?

Overnight care typically runs from late evening through early morning. I tend to your baby through the night — feeding support, soothing, diaper changes — so you and your partner can rest. I arrive quietly, keep your home calm, and leave you with notes about how the night went.

How far in advance should I book?

I recommend reaching out during pregnancy if possible — ideally in the second or third trimester — so we can plan thoughtfully and reserve dates. I do my best to take on families closer to their due date when my schedule allows.

Do you work with families of all structures?

Yes. I serve single parents, partnered parents, queer and trans families, blended families, and every shape of family. My care is rooted in respect for every path.

How do we start working together?

Begin with a brief consultation. From there, I'll send a thoughtful intake form, and we'll shape a plan that meets your family where you are. Everything moves at your pace.
